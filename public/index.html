              How to play

              Players take turns "drawing" a secret word on a pixel grid. But here's the catch, they must use words to draw instead of a pencil.
              1. The host creates a game and shares the link. Everyone hits Ready, then the host starts.
              2. The artist gets a secret word to draw on a pixel grid. The grid is 8×8 by default; but the the game host can pick 9×9, 16×16 or 17×17. Odd sizes have a true center cell, which makes things a bit easier.
              3. Instead of drawing with a pencil, Type a command: color [shape] [size] cells (e.g. lime circle big 36). Cells are numbered from 1 in the top-left corner, left to right (up to 64 on 8×8).
                • red 7: paint cell 7 red
                • blue all: fill the whole grid (great for backgrounds)
                • lime square big 28: a big lime square centered on 28
                • banana circle 20 / banana ring 20: a filled circle, or just its outline
                • black cells 1, 6, 11: paint just those cells (use commas to stamp a shape on several cells: red plus 10, 30)
                • black line 1 to 57: a straight line between two cells
                • green row 30 / green col 3: the whole row or column
              4. Shapes: dot, square, box, circle, ring, plus, x, line, row, col, diamond, triangle. Sizes: small (3×3), big (5×5), huge (9×9), or a number: beige square 3 50 is a 3×3 square on cell 50. Shapes stop at the edge of the grid.
              5. Colors: any color word works, even things like lava. Made-up words get a random color. 🎲
              6. Oops? Use clear as the color to erase cells (clear 12, clear row 9), or clear all to wipe the grid.
              7. Stuck? The drawer can skip to a new word until someone guesses it. The timer keeps running.
              8. Everyone else types guesses. Wrong guesses show up for everyone; right ones stay secret.
              9. Scoring: a correct guess earns 50–100 points (faster earns more; with no timer, earlier guessers earn more). The drawer gets +25 for each person who guesses it.
              10. With no timer, the turn ends when everyone has guessed, or when the drawer or host hits End turn.
              11. If someone's connection drops (say their phone locks), the game pauses for up to 2 minutes and picks up where it left off when they're back.
              12. 💾 Save any drawing during the reveal, or the whole gallery at the end of the game.
              13. The turn ends when time runs out or everyone has guessed it.
